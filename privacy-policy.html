Overview

This site — avisingh.studio — is a personal portfolio belonging to Avi Singh, a web and brand designer based in Melbourne, Australia. This policy explains what data is collected when you visit this site and how it is used.

What We Collect

This site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous data about how visitors use the site. If you accept cookies, Google Analytics may collect:

  • Approximate location (country and city level — not precise)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • How you arrived at the site (e.g. search engine, direct link)

No personally identifiable information (name, email address, etc.) is collected through analytics. If you choose to contact me directly via email, that information is handled only for the purposes of responding to your enquiry.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets the following cookies when analytics is accepted:

  • _ga — Distinguishes unique users. Expires after 2 years.
  • _gid — Distinguishes unique users. Expires after 24 hours.
  • _ga_* — Persists session state. Expires after 2 years.

If you decline cookies, no analytics cookies are set and your visit is not tracked.

How We Use This Data

Analytics data is used solely to understand how visitors find and use this site — for example, which pages are most viewed or where traffic comes from. This helps improve the site over time. The data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party beyond Google Analytics itself.

Your Choices

You can opt out of analytics tracking at any time:

  • Decline cookies when the banner appears on your first visit
  • Clear your browser cookies, then decline when prompted again
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. Google's handling of data collected through Analytics is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Get in touch: avi@avisingh.studio

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
